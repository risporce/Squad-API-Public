Supercell will not allow a repository to have their game assets uploaded online, so you have to do the work of getting them and uncompressing them unfortunately.
Here's the needed uncompressed files in this directory:

avatar_stats.csv
characters.csv
end_game_milestones.csv
exp_levels.csv
regions.csv
resources.csv
skins.csv
spells.csv
texts.csv
variables.csv

to decompress these files, you can use a public tool such as this one: https://github.com/PhoenixFire6934/SC-tool

if you're unable to get them, dm me on discord or telegram @risporce, and i'll be happy to give them to you
